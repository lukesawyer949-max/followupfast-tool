Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Short version: we collect your email to run the free trial and to contact you about FollowUpFast. What you type into the tool is sent to an AI service to write your messages, and we don't keep it. Your saved leads stay in your own browser. We don't sell your information.

Who we are

FollowUpFast is operated by WaveTek Studio LLC, doing business as FollowUpFast ("we", "us"). You can reach us at hello@getfollowupfast.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address, which you enter to start the free trial.
  • Usage information for the trial: how many free generations you've used, when you last used one, and whether you've subscribed.
  • A scrambled (hashed) version of your network address, used only to limit how many free trials can start from one network. We don't store your raw IP address in our database.
  • Standard hosting logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP address, browser type and time of each request), used for security and troubleshooting.

What you type into the tool

The business name, your name, your callback number, the customer's name, the job type, the quote amount and any extra details you type are sent to our server and passed to an AI provider (Anthropic) so it can write your messages. If you add a Google review link, it is sent to our server only so we can add it to your thank-you message, and it is not shared with the AI provider. Your review link is also saved in your browser. We don't store these details or the messages it writes. The customer's phone number and email are not sent to us. They stay in your browser and are only used to open your own messaging app. Please don't enter sensitive information such as payment card numbers, passwords, government ID numbers or health information.

The customer names and details you enter belong to your business. You're responsible for having a legitimate reason to use them.

What stays in your browser

The leads you track in the Lead tracker, including customer names, phone numbers, emails, notes, quote amounts and follow-up dates, along with the "Money won back" totals, your own email (so you don't have to retype it) and your install-prompt choice, are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. We don't receive them. Clearing your browser data removes them, and the installed app may keep a separate list from your browser, so use "Download leads (CSV)" to keep a copy.

How we use your information

  • To run the free trial and enforce its limit.
  • To unlock your account if you subscribe.
  • To email you about FollowUpFast, such as your trial, product updates and offers. Every marketing email will include a way to opt out.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We don't sell your personal information and we don't use it for advertising.

Who else handles your information

  • Supabase stores trial records (email, usage count, hashed network address).
  • Vercel hosts the website and keeps standard server logs.
  • Anthropic processes the details you enter to generate your messages.
  • Stripe processes subscription payments. Your card details go directly to Stripe, and we never see or store them.

These providers handle data under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. We use your browser's local storage for the features described above.

How long we keep it

We keep trial records for as long as they're useful to run the service and prevent abuse, and we delete them when you ask.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or opt out of marketing emails, by writing to hello@getfollowupfast.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time. Some states give residents additional rights, and we'll honor valid requests.

Children

FollowUpFast is for business use and isn't directed to anyone under 18.

Security

We use access controls and encryption in transit, and our database isn't open to the public. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Material changes will be announced on this page or by email.
